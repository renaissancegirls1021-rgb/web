This is the Duchamp piece!

Fountain

A landmark of “Readymade” art. The work consists of nothing more than a mass-produced men’s urinal turned on its side and signed with a name, yet its bizarre nature forced society to confront the question: “Can this truly be called art?” The original has been lost, but because the work is inherently easy to reproduce, several replicas still exist today.

Who is Duchamp?

Marcel Duchamp

A French artist who had an immeasurable influence on 20th-century art through the concept of the “Readymade.” His work Fountain, which displayed a mass-produced urinal as it was, is still discussed more than a century later as a symbol of modern art. In recent years, scholars have also revisited the possibility that fellow Dada artist Elsa von Freytag-Loringhoven may have been involved in the ideas or production behind some of his works, and research on the subject continues today.
