Swing

A woman gracefully riding on a swing. The man pushing the swing on the right is her husband, while the figure hiding in the bushes below on the left is her lover. Details scattered throughout the painting—such as the shoe slipping from her foot and the small dog nearby—hint at flirtation and tangled romance. Overflowing with playful charm and sensuality, the work feels almost like a romantic comedy brought to life.

Who is Fragonard?

Jean Honoré Fragonard

A painter who came to symbolize the final flowering of Rococo art. Beloved by aristocrats for his extravagantly vibrant colors, lighthearted compositions, and elegant romantic subjects, he is often considered the ultimate culmination of the Rococo style itself. However, as time passed and the French Revolution transformed society’s values, his popularity rapidly faded, and he is said to have spent his later years in poverty. He is also known for the famous anecdote that he collapsed shortly after eating ice cream.
