This is the Rembrandt piece!

The Night Watch

This is a so-called "group portrait" of members of the Dutch Matchlock Guild. Unlike portraits of the time, which tended to be impersonal, like passport photographs, this one dramatically depicts members about to set out. Originally a daytime scene, the surface became darkened by chemicals applied to it, and at some point it came to be called "The Night Watchman."

Who is Rembrandt?

Rembrandt van Rijn

The "magician of light and shadow" was born in the United Republic of the Netherlands (now the Kingdom of the Netherlands). His skillful manipulation of light and dark, his depictions of even the inner lives of people, make him a true representative of the Baroque period, and his name shines brightly in the history of Western art. He was also known as a spendthrift, and despite achieving early fame, he struggled financially. He left behind a large number of self-portraits, making him one of the painters whose appearance is easily understood.
