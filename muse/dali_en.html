This is the Dali piece!

The Persistence of Memory

One of the most famous works of surrealist painting. Though it is a small work, it is packed with essential elements of Dali, such as his feelings for his hometown of Catalonia, his complexes, and his interest in physics. Dali said that the famous "Soft Clock" was inspired by melted Camembert cheese and was completed in about two hours.

The rock in the upper right is a cliff from my homeland, Catalonia—and the ants on the left are a bug I hate. In other words, this painting is just like its title: a collection of Dalí’s memories!

Who is Dali?

Salvador Dali

A leading painter of 20th-century Surrealism. He established the image of “Surrealism” by meticulously rendering landscapes where dreams and reality intermingled with astonishing technique. Beyond his art, he famously declared himself a genius and attracted attention with eccentric behavior and attire, becoming notorious as a scandalous celebrity. However, as documented in his autobiography, his foundation was rooted in reverence for classical art, and his mastery of depiction was underpinned by rigorous study.
