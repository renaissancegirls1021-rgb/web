This is the Zeuxis piece!

Zeuxis Choosing his Models for the Image of Helen from among the Girls of Croton

Zeuxis was commissioned to paint Helen, the legendary beauty of Greek mythology. To create an ideal beauty, he chose several young women as models and combined the most beautiful features of each into a single figure. The illustration was painted by the 18th-century French artist Vincent, and the story of Zeuxis has been passed down through later works of art.

Who is Zeuxis?

Zeuxis

A legendary painter said to have lived in ancient Greece. None of his works survive, but his exceptional skill was praised in various ancient writings. He was especially known for realistic painting; one famous tale says that birds tried to peck at grapes he had painted, mistaking them for real ones. Late in life, he painted a goddess using an old woman as his model. Her comical appearance made him laugh uncontrollably, and he is said to have died laughing.
