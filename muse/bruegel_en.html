This is the Bruegel piece!

Tower of Babel

A work based on an episode from the Old Testament. Once upon a time, all people on earth spoke the same language. With their advanced technology and strong unity, they attempted to build a tower that would reach the heavens, but their endeavor incurred God’s wrath. God scattered their languages, and when they could no longer communicate with one another, they eventually abandoned the construction of the tower. This work depicts the construction site, and the meticulously rendered gestures of the people reveal the keen observational eye characteristic of Pieter Bruegel.

Who is Bruegel?

Pieter Bruegel the Elder

A painter active in the 16th-century Netherlands （present-day Belgium）. He was known as “Peasant Bruegel” for his close association with farmers and his vivid depictions of their lives. His meticulously detailed works depicting seasonal landscapes, village festivals, and peasant customs are also invaluable historical documents for understanding the society and daily life of the period. The Bruegel family was a renowned dynasty that produced many painters; to distinguish him from a later painter of the same name, he is often referred to as “Bruegel the Elder.”
