This is the Bonnard piece!

Nude in the Bath and Small Dog

An interior painting of Bonnard’s wife, Marthe, bathing. Marthe suffered from illness and bathed frequently, sometimes spending most of the day in the tub. Bonnard painted her again and again, and Marthe appears in about a third of the more than 2,000 works he produced over his lifetime.

Artists who often painted intimate domestic scenes are called “Intimists.” I’m one of their most famous muses!

Who is Bonnard?

Pierre Bonnard

One of the central members of the Nabis. His father was a government official, and he grew up privileged, even earning a law degree. Yet he was gentle and unhurried, sometimes taking years to finish a single painting. After meeting Marthe, who became his wife, his life gained new color, and he repeatedly used her as a model. In his youth, he became known for poster design and even influenced Toulouse-Lautrec, a leading figure of Art Nouveau.
