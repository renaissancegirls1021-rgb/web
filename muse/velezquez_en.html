This is the Velezquez piece!

Las Meninas

The title means "Ladies in Waiting" in Spanish. Several people, including Princess Margarita in the center, are looking toward the viewer, while Velázquez, on the far left, is holding a brush and observing the viewer. Reflected in the mirror in the background are King Philip IV and his queen. Indeed, this painting is painted from the perspective of the king, whose portrait Velázquez is painting.

That red cross painted on the chest of the painter standing on the far left? It’s the emblem of a historic Spanish order of knights, and it was considered a very great honor.

Who is Velezquez?

Diego Velázquez

A leading court painter of Spain's Golden Age, he served Philip IV and painted portraits of a wide range of subjects, including royalty, nobility, and even jesters. His portraits, characterized by their dignified brushwork that vividly brought out the presence and humanity of his subjects, stood apart from the works of other painters and strongly influenced later artists such as Goya and Picasso. He held a high position within the royal court and, in addition to being an artist, also worked as a diplomat, among other diverse activities. It is said that he died from overwork due to his extremely busy schedule.
