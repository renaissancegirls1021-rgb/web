This is the Giorgione piece!

The Tempest

One of Giorgione’s most famous surviving works. A soldier watches a gypsy woman nursing a baby. Its subject remains unclear, inspiring many interpretations. It is commonly called The Tempest after the stormy weather in the background. Unusually for its time, the painting emphasizes the landscape over the figures and is sometimes considered an early landscape painting.

This painting was commissioned by a Venetian nobleman. One theory is that its mysterious nature comes from being made for a private collection. So, no matter how much you think about it… it’s MUDA!

Who is Giorgione?

Giorgione

An Italian painter active in Venice during the High Renaissance. Few works or records of his life survive, leaving much about him a mystery. Yet the poetic atmosphere and distinctive charm of works attributed to him suggest he was as gifted as contemporary biographies claim. He strongly influenced later painters, including fellow Venetian Renaissance artist Titian. Since their styles are similar, however, some paintings are difficult to attribute to one or the other.
