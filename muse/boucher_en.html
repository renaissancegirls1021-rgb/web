This is the Boucher piece!

Diana Bathing

The scene depicts Diana, the Greek goddess of the hunt, bathing with her attendant nymphs. Although based on the tragedy depicted in Ovid’s Metamorphoses, this work presents a strikingly bright and serene atmosphere in contrast. From the nymphs’ soft skin and finely rendered fabrics to the game and hunting dogs, every detail is carefully depicted, showcasing Boucher’s exceptional skill.

Bathing women have long been a popular subject. Even the Impressionist Renoir was inspired by my works and painted bathing scenes again and again. How amusing that our tastes should meet across the ages.

Who is Boucher?

François Boucher

A leading French Rococo painter, Boucher expanded the style after the death of Watteau, one of its pioneers. He became known for glamorous, sensual scenes from mythology, though his romantic style was sometimes criticized as immoral. He also embraced chinoiserie, leaving works depicting Chinese figures based on reference materials. Boucher was related to Neoclassical master David, who hoped to study under him when young.
