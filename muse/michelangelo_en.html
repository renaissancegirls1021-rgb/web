This is the Michelangelo piece!

The Pietà of Saint Peter's

This masterpiece was created by Michelangelo when he was 23 years old.

Pieta is an Italian word meaning "pity," and refers to a work of art depicting the Virgin Mary mourning the death of Christ. The carefully rendered facial expressions and textures of the bodies and clothing of the figures make you forget that they are made of marble.

Incidentally, it is the only work to bear Michelangelo's signature.

Who is Michelangelo?

Michelangelo Buonarroti

He was a multi-talented genius who represented Italy during the Renaissance period.

He displayed talent in a wide range of fields, including sculpture, painting, and architecture, and his ceiling paintings in the Sistine Chapel are sometimes called a miracle of mankind. He served the Pope and the Medici family throughout his life, and while he sometimes clashed with them, he was also a professional who left behind some amazing works.

However, there are also records that he had a very difficult personality, and it is said that he suffered a lifelong complex about his crooked nose, which he suffered from after being hit in a fight.
