This is the David piece!

Bonaparte Crossing the Alps

This work depicts Napoleon Bonaparte crossing the Saint-Bernard Pass in the Alps to head towards Italy in 1800. There is an anecdote about Napoleon's request to change from riding a mule, which was a strong horse, to riding a horse instead.

Incidentally, the title is "Bonaparte" because in Europe it is customary to refer to emperors by their surname before ascending to the throne and by their given name after ascending to the throne.

Who is David?

Jacques-Louis David

A painter representing French neoclassicism, his portrait of Napoleon, a painter to the royal court, is surely one you've seen in textbooks. His historical paintings, based on a strict classicism that is almost cold-hearted, influenced many of his students.

He was also an active politician, and left his mark in the history of the French Revolution as a Jacobin who promoted the revolution. He died in exile in Belgium, but was not allowed to be buried in France due to his support for the execution of Louis XVI.
