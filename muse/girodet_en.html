This is the Girodet piece!

The Sleep of Endymion

Selene, the moon goddess of Greek mythology, was riding across the night sky in her lunar chariot when she fell in love at first sight with a mortal hunter named Endymion, who was sleeping outdoors. Night after night, Selene approached him and admired his sleeping face. Unlike the immortal goddess, however, Endymion continued to age. Unable to bear the thought of losing his beauty, Selene pleaded with Zeus, and Endymion was granted eternal youth and beauty in exchange for an endless sleep.

I was getting sooo bored, so I tried painting in a style completely different from Master David's. And somehow he ended up liking it even more. Funny how that works, isn't it~?

Who is Girodet?

Girodet de Roussy-Trioson

A French painter of the Neoclassical era and one of Jacques-Louis David's most talented pupils. Although he inherited his master's exceptional draftsmanship, Girodet developed a distinctive style filled with dreamy, mysterious imagery, making him one of the earliest artists to anticipate Romanticism while remaining rooted in Neoclassicism. Although relatively little is known about his life, his innovative approach had a lasting influence on French painting and helped bridge the gap between two major artistic movements.
