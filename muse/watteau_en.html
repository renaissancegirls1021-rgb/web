This is the Watteau piece!

The Embarkation for Cythera

A group of lovers making a pilgrimage to Cythera, the island sacred to Venus and associated with love. It is a major example of the fête galante, a Rococo genre depicting people enjoying leisure in pastoral settings. The work brought Watteau recognition and admission to the Academy, leading him to create another version of the same subject and composition.

Who is Watteau?

Antoine Watteau

A leading French painter of early Rococo. He rose to fame young with fêtes galantes, scenes of elegant couples at leisure or in love, capturing the world of the aristocracy. He died of tuberculosis at 36, but his delicate style influenced Boucher and Fragonard. His name also lives on in fashion terms such as the Watteau gown and Watteau back.
