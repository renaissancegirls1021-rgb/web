This is the Varo piece!

Creation of the Birds

A woman (presumably) clad in feathers is painting. A bird, painted with starlight pigments, escapes the paper and flies off into the starry sky again. Varo studied the occult with his close friend, the painter Leonora Carrington, and the work is peppered with motifs alluding to alchemy and magic, creating an unparalleled sense of mystery.

At the time, I had taken refuge in Mexico to escape the ravages of war. This is a piece that holds particularly deep meaning for me, as it was created just as my work was beginning to take shape.

Who is Varo?

Remedios Varo

A Spanish-born Surrealist painter, she gained prominence in France and Mexico for her fantastical and mystical style. Her unique worldview, inspired by alchemy and science, is both fantastical and intellectually stimulating. At the same time, she possessed a practical side, taking on commercial work such as posters and calendars during periods of financial hardship. She was also known for her many romantic affairs, and there is even an anecdote about artists getting into a physical altercation over one of her relationships.
