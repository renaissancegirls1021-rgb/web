This is the Courbet piece!

A Burial at Ornans

This painting depicts the funeral of a relative held in Ornans, a small town in France, Courbet's hometown. The work, which portrays an ordinary scene, the funeral of an unknown person, as if it were a momentous event, drew criticism from critics. At the time, such large canvases were only permitted for religious or historical paintings.

Who is Courbet?

Gustave Courbet

A French painter who established Realism in the nineteenth century. Courbet rejected mythology and idealized beauty, depicting ordinary people and everyday life. “I have never seen an angel, so I will not paint one,” he valued direct observation over fantasy. His bold style challenged academic traditions and caused controversy. After rejection from the Exposition Universelle, he created his own exhibition, a landmark in independent art. Though handsome in youth, he became heavier later in life.
