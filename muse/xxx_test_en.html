This is the Rembrandt piece!

The Night Watch

This is a so-called "group portrait" of members of the Dutch Matchlock Guild. Unlike portraits of the time, which tended to be impersonal, like passport photographs, this one dramatically depicts members about to set out. Originally a daytime scene, the surface became darkened by chemicals applied to it, and at some point it came to be called "The Night Watchman."

Who is Rembrandt?

Rembrandt van Rijn

Born in the Dutch Republic (present-day Kingdom of the Netherlands), he was a "master of light and shadow." His skillful manipulation of light and shadow, and his ability to depict even the inner lives of his subjects, made him a quintessential Baroque artist, and his name shines brightly in the history of Western art. On the other hand, he was also known as a spendthrift, and despite achieving fame early on, he suffered financially. He left behind a large number of self-portraits, making him one of the painters whose appearance is very easy to discern.
