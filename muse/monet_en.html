This is the Monet piece!

Impression, Sunrise

This masterpiece by Monet is said to be the origin of the name "Impressionism".
It depicts a view of the port of Le Havre in northwestern France, painted with rough brushstrokes, and is famously criticized as being "better than half-finished wallpaper".

It was not well-received for a while after its release, and it wasn't considered a masterpiece until some time after Impressionism was recognized by the public.

Who is Monet?

Claude Monet

Monet is a painter who represents Impressionism and, by extension, modern French art.

He created beautiful works such as "Impression, Sunrise" and "Water Lilies" by focusing on capturing the shifting light and atmosphere, and the rippling water surface with his brush.

His style of painting, which seems enveloped in gentle light, is widely loved, but his life was by no means smooth sailing, and he was also a struggling painter who continued to pursue contemporary art amidst hardships.

Monet's final home and garden remain in Giverny, France, where you can see the water lilies he loved so much.
