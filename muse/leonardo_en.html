The Last Supper

This work depicts the scene from the New Testament in which Christ shares his final meal with his apostles. The vividly portrayed figures, arranged with rhythmic balance, and the background constructed through linear perspective are excellent examples of Renaissance artistic ideals. Despite suffering severe damage throughout history, it has survived for nearly five centuries—a true miracle of a painting.

Who is Leonardo?

Leonardo da Vinci

One of the most influential artists of the Renaissance, Leonardo was a true "Renaissance Man" whose talents extended beyond painting into science, engineering, and architecture. He introduced innovative techniques such as linear perspective and sfumato, creating masterpieces like the Mona Lisa and The Last Supper. Leonardo also left behind designs for flying machines, tanks, and even devices resembling contact lenses, revealing an imagination far ahead of his era. Though often called "da Vinci," he is increasingly referred to simply as "Leonardo" today.
