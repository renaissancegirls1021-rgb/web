This is the Matisse piece!

The Dessert: Harmony in Red

One of Matisse's greatest masterpieces, this painting of a woman in an interior marks the starting point of his later series of interior scenes. As the title Harmony suggests, it explores the delicate balance between color, flatness, and spatial depth. By the time it was painted, Matisse and his fellow artists had already been labeled "Fauves" ("wild beasts") for their supposedly crude style. Yet despite its daring use of color, the work conveys an atmosphere of remarkable calm and harmony.

Who is Matisse?

Henri Matisse

A leading French painter of Fauvism, Henri Matisse helped define the movement alongside Vlaminck and Derain through his bold use of vivid color. Even after Fauvism ended, he continued exploring new artistic styles. When illness left him unable to paint, he created his famous paper cut-outs from colored paper. His creativity made him one of the most influential artists of the twentieth century. A famous anecdote tells of one of his cut-outs being displayed upside down without anyone noticing.
