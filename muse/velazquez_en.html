This is the Velazquez piece!

Las Meninas

The title means “The Ladies-in-Waiting” in Spanish, and the painting depicts a scene from the Spanish royal court. At the center stands the young Infanta Margarita, surrounded by her attendants and servants, while Velázquez himself appears at the far left holding a paintbrush. In the mirror at the back, the reflections of King Philip IV and his queen can be seen, revealing that the composition is constructed from the king’s point of view.

That red cross painted on the chest of the painter standing on the far left? It’s the emblem of a historic Spanish order of knights, and it was considered a very great honor.

Who is Velazquez?

Diego Velázquez

A court painter who came to symbolize Spain’s Golden Age. Serving under King Philip IV, he painted not only royalty and aristocrats, but also jesters, workers, and many other kinds of people. His portraits, with their refined brushwork and vivid sense of human presence, stood apart from those of other painters and later had a tremendous influence on Spanish artists such as Francisco Goya and Pablo Picasso. He also held a high position within the royal court and worked as a diplomat in addition to being an artist, leading an extremely busy life. Some even say he died from overwork.
