This is the Haring piece!

Crack is Wack

Literally translated, "Cocaine is lame." This mural was created to protest the widespread use of cocaine and its delayed regulation.

Because it was initially painted without permission, Haring was arrested for vandalism, but due to public outcry, his sentence was reduced and he was later officially commissioned by the Parks Department.

It underwent extensive restoration in 2012.

Who is Haring?

Keith Haring

He was a pioneer of pop art in New York in the 1980s. He began his career with subway graffiti and quickly gained popularity for his bold lines and bouncy characters.

His bright and humorous style is widely loved by people of all ages, and is printed on T-shirts and other items. However, his themes, which primarily raise political and social issues, are quite heavy.

He was openly gay and passed away from AIDS at the young age of 31, but his iconic figures continue to dance on street corners to this day.
