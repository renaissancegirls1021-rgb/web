This is the Millais piece!

Ophelia

This painting depicts a woman who appears in Shakespeare's play "Hamlet." When Ophelia learns that Hamlet, the man she loves, has murdered her father, she goes mad with grief and drowns in the river while singing. This is a masterpiece that beautifully depicts the transience and beauty of a woman who meets a tragic death, with meticulous depictions of nature typical of the Pre-Raphaelites.

Who is Millais?

Sir John Everett Millais

He was a genius who became the youngest person ever admitted to the British Academy, and one of the central figures in the founding of the Pre-Raphaelite Brotherhood. His paintings, which were the result of meticulous observation of his motifs, still exude a timeless beauty. Although he was a rebellious painter in his youth, he softened his attitude in later years and switched to a style that was more popular with the masses, achieving success and even becoming president of the Academy. However, at the same time, the Pre-Raphaelite Brotherhood, which was based on the motto of rebellion against authority, also came to an end.
