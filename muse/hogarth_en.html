This is the Hogarth piece!

Marriage A-la-Mode

“Marriage A-la-Mode” means “fashionable marriage.” This six-part series follows a young couple whose marriage, driven by money and status, ends in ruin. Works that exposed human depravity to educate the public were common at the time, and Hogarth was a master of the genre.

That black mark on the husband's neck? It's a symptom of syphilis, a dreadful disease. There are plenty of other clues hinting at this couple's fate, too. Don't miss a single one~

Who is Hogarth?

William Hogarth

A painter who helped lay the foundation for 18th-century British art. Influenced by French Rococo, he established a unique style through satirical works about the aristocracy and society. His biting scenes won over the public and helped advance British art. After his own works were widely pirated, he also campaigned for copyright protection, contributing to the creation of laws that became a basis for modern copyright.
