This is the Munch piece!

The Scream

Under a distorted, blood-red sky, a man covers his ears and endures a scream that pierces nature. Originally a frightening painting, its somewhat humorous appearance has led to it being used in various parodies, making it extremely well-known in Japan. He has repeatedly painted this work using oils as well as pastels and tempera, and there are a total of five of them.

Gyoeeehhh! In the top-left corner of this painting—written in tiny pencil—it says, ‘Could only have been painted by a madman’!!!

Who is Munch?

Edvard Munch

A national painter whose image has even been featured on Norwegian banknotes. Despite facing a lack of recognition for his art and experiencing mental instability, he continued to paint, even moving to Germany, and eventually achieved high acclaim during his lifetime. He was a painter of great effort and perseverance. While he is often perceived as only painting gruesome pictures, such as "The Scream," which express his own madness, he actually left behind many portraits, landscapes, and depictions of working people.
