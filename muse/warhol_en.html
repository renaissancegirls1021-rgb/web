This is the Warhol piece!

Campbell's Soup Cans

A work made up of rows of printed Campbell's Soup cans. In America, canned Campbell's Soup was an everyday product found in just about every supermarket, and Warhol himself reportedly lived on it during his struggling years. By repeating such an ordinary household item over and over, he challenged the world with the idea that even the most commonplace objects could become art.

Who is Warhol?

Andy Warhol

An iconic American artist and one of the central figures of Pop Art. By turning everyday objects such as canned goods, consumer products, and celebrity portraits into artistic subjects, he explored both the glamour and the emptiness of modern popular culture. His eccentric public persona was as carefully crafted as his art, and his unusual remarks and performances constantly attracted attention. Believed to have been deeply self-conscious about his appearance, he underwent cosmetic procedures and adopted his trademark silver wig, ultimately transforming his own image into one of Pop Art's most recognizable icons.
