This is the Giacometti piece!

The Walking Man I

One of Giacometti's masterpieces. He created six works on the same theme. The figure, stripped down to its absolute physical limit, appears so frail it seems like the smallest possible unit of “human existence”—reliably fragile yet somehow proud. It is sometimes linked to the philosophical movement of existentialism, popular in the 19th and 20th centuries.

Who is Giacometti?

Alberto Giacometti

A leading Swiss sculptor and painter of the 20th century. After embracing Surrealism in his youth, he arrived at his own unique style. His sculptures, carved so slenderly they seemed ready to topple with the slightest push, resonated as reflections of the loneliness and anxiety that came with the postwar era. They came to be described as “existentialist,” a term championed by philosophers like Sartre. His brother Diego devotedly assisted him as an assistant and model, later achieving great success himself as a sculptor and furniture designer.
