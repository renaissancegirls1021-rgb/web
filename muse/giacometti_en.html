This is the Giacometti piece!

The Walking Man I

One of Giacometti’s representative works. He created six versions based on the same theme. The figure, stripped down to the bare minimum, seems almost like the very image of a human quietly pressing onward through a vast empty space. Because of its unique presence, the work is often associated with “Existentialism,” a philosophical movement that spread throughout the 20th century.

I was making it to display at a bank in New York, but I took too long, and by the time I realized it, the deal had fallen through. …What a shame.

Who is Giacometti?

Alberto Giacometti

A Swiss sculptor and painter who became a defining artist of 20th-century Switzerland. Influenced by Surrealism in his youth, he developed a unique style of thin, elongated figures standing alone in empty spaces. Their stark appearance captured postwar anxieties and earned acclaim. His work is often associated with Existentialism, linked to thinkers such as Jean-Paul Sartre. His younger brother Diego supported him as an assistant and model, later becoming a sculptor and furniture designer.
