This is the Bosch piece!

The Garden of Earthly Delights

A triptych and one of Bosch’s largest works. The left panel shows Paradise, the right depicts Hell, and the center shows the Garden of Earthly Delights. The bright central panel portrays people indulging in pleasure, who will eventually fall into the Hell depicted on the right. Its surreal scenes, with enormous fruit and bizarre demons, were unlike anything else of its time and later inspired the Surrealists some 400 years later.

The sides of this painting are hinged doors, you see. When you close them, you can see the Earth being created by God. This world and Hell are two sides of the same coin, you know…

Who is Bosch?

Hieronymus Bosch

A leading painter of the Northern Renaissance. Hieronymus Bosch was not his real name; it is thought to derive from his hometown, ’s-Hertogenbosch. Little is known about his life, and only around 30 works survive, adding to his mystery. A member of a local Christian brotherhood, he painted religious works filled with bizarre monsters and fantastical visions of Hell. He is considered a pioneer of fantastical art and one of the most unusual figures in Western art history.
