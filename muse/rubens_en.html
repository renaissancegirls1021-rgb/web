This is the Rubens piece!

The Elevation of the Cross/The Descent from the Cross

A series of paintings depicting Christ being crucified and then taken down from the cross. In Japan, it is probably Rubens’s most famous work thanks to being known as “the painting Nello saw at the end of A Dog of Flanders.” The depiction of Christ’s tragic yet powerful body draws heavily from studies of ancient Roman art, especially works like the Laocoön Group, giving the painting an overwhelming sense of force and intensity.

Ohohohoho! This painting is what’s called a “triptych,” which means the side panels swing open like doors! Truly magnificent art deserves to be saved for a grand reveal, after all~!

Who is Rubens?

Peter Paul Rubens

A master who came to define Baroque art, and one of the most successful painters in the history of Western art. Influenced by Italian classical sculpture and Renaissance painting, his dynamic and brilliantly colorful works earned immense popularity both at home and abroad. As his fame grew, so did the flood of commissions, which he handled together with a large number of assistants in his workshop. At the same time, he was also active as a diplomat, traveling across Europe and even taking part in peace negotiations. A truly extraordinary man of many talents.
