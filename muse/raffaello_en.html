This is the Raffaello piece!

The School of Athens

One of the monumental frescoes decorating the Vatican Palace. Plato, Aristotle, and many of ancient Greece's greatest philosophers gather here in lively discussion, expressing the pursuit of truth through philosophy. Its lifelike figures, masterful perspective, and magnificent architecture make it one of the finest achievements of Renaissance art.

Some of the figures are modeled after people I admired. Take Plato, pointing toward the heavens in the center-left—doesn't he look just like Leonardo da Vinci? Hehe... A little playful touch never hurts.

Who is Raffaello?

Raffaello Santi

One of the greatest painters of the High Renaissance. Rising to fame at a young age, he produced countless masterpieces while leading a large workshop. Although he died at just thirty-seven, his perfectly balanced compositions and elegant figures became an ideal of Western art, influencing generations of artists. An early biography claimed he died because of excessive love affairs, but today that story is generally considered unlikely.
