Millais

Back when the Pre-Raphaelite Brotherhood burst onto the scene, Britain was right in the middle of the super-hype Victorian era! The Industrial Revolution was going full throttle, and Britain was basically ruling the world! So of course we Pre-Raphaelite members were like, "This is our chance—we're gonna make it big!" We jumped in with total confidence and all the energy we had! ♥

Point①
They looked to art created
before Raphael as their ideal.

Millais

Back then, art schools in Britain acted like, "Raphael is the ultimate peak of art!" Seriously? Why should that be the only answer!? We figured the art before Raphael had a much purer, more genuine spirit. So instead of copying Renaissance ideals, we turned our eyes to the artists who came before him!

Point②
A bold new art movement
founded by young artists.

Rossetti

The coolest thing about us Pre-Raphaelites? We were basically all students! We were young, fearless, and totally ready to shake things up. That's exactly why we could go all in on making a brand-new kind of art! Peak youth energy, right!? ★

Point③
Though the movement
eventually faded,
it had a lasting influence
on British art.

Hunt

The Pre-Raphaelite Brotherhood started with everyone chasing the same dream. At first, our fresh ideas attracted a lot of attention—but as time passed, everyone began following their own path, and the group naturally drifted apart. We didn't stay together for very long. Honestly, we were all kind of hopeless in our own ways... though that's exactly what I liked about us.

This is the Pre-Raphaelite Brotherhood piece!

Ophelia

This painting depicts a female character from Shakespeare's Hamlet. Following the Pre-Raphaelite belief in observing nature with absolute fidelity, every leaf is rendered with astonishing precision. By combining literary subjects with meticulous realism, the work explores the depths of human emotion and spirituality, helping pave the way for the later Symbolist movement.
