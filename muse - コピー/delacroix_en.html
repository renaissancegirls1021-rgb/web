This is the Delacroix piece!

Liberty Leading the People

This work is based on the July Revolution of 1830 in France. The bare-chested woman in the center is Marianne, the goddess who symbolizes the nation of France itself. The man in the top hat is said to be modeled after Delacroix himself, but this is uncertain. This work, which depicts current events with bold, rough brushstrokes, is a symbol of Romanticism.

The July Revolution was when people rose up against King Charles X… you know, a re-vo-lu-tion? The building you see in the back is Notre-Dame Cathedral, where it all took place.

Who is Delacroix?

Eugène Delacroix

A painter who symbolizes French Romanticism. He excelled at using rich colors and vigorous brushstrokes, and was adept at depicting current events with emotion. While he often clashed with the powerful Neoclassical painters of the time, he also possessed a shrewdness that respected tradition. His rebellious spirit greatly influenced later painters and laid the foundation for modern art, leading to Impressionism. His diaries, kept over several years, reveal the painter's struggle with art despite his frail health.
