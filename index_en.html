MUSE

The Muses are goddesses of the arts
who protect and guide artists.
Through the Muses' divine inspiration
bestowed upon artists,
countless magnificent masterpieces were born.

Art Styles & Art Movements

In the world of art,
every era and region has had its own
popular styles, ideas,
and ways of expression.


At Renaissance Girls,
groups of muses who share the same passion—
“I want to paint like this!” or
“I want to spread this kind of expression!”—
are defined as
“art styles” and “art movements.”
